View Modes & Options

Proposed explorer controls: four view modes (list / detail / grid / large grid), a sort+filter sheet, a show-hidden toggle, and image thumbnails where decodable. App-bar icons toggle the view mode (▤) and open the options sheet (⇅). These are static mockups to agree on the design before implementation — not final visuals.
